FAQs

Study Abroad: Common Questions Answered.

1. How much does it cost to study abroad?

The total cost depends on your destination, university, course and city. Plan for both tuition fees and living expenses.
Explore Cost Calculator

2. Can I get a scholarship or education loan?

Yes, you may be eligible for scholarships based on your academic profile and university criteria. Education loans are also available through eligible lenders, subject to their approval and terms.

3. What is the difference between Regular and Fast Track Admission?

With Regular Admission, you start your degree at an overseas university from the first semester. With selected Fast Track programmes, you begin online in India for 8–12 months before continuing at a partner university abroad, subject to credit-transfer approval.

4. Can I work while studying abroad?

Yes, eligible international students can work part-time while studying. Permitted working hours and conditions depend on the country's student visa rules.

5. Can I work after completing my degree abroad?

Yes, eligible graduates may apply for post-study work opportunities in certain countries. Visa duration and eligibility depend on the destination country's immigration policy.

6. Can I get a post-study work visa after completing a Fast Track programme?

Yes, you may be eligible for a post-study work visa after completing your Fast Track programme and qualifying overseas degree. Eligibility depends on the destination country's immigration rules, course, university and other visa requirements.

7. How long does the admission process take?

The timeline varies by university, destination and intake. Starting early gives you enough time to complete applications, arrange documents and meet admission deadlines.
