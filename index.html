FAQs

Study Abroad: Common Questions Answered.

1. Can I study abroad after 12th?

Yes. You can apply for Bachelor's and other undergraduate programmes after Class 12, depending on your academic marks, subject requirements and university eligibility.

2. Can I study abroad after graduation?

Yes. You can apply for Master's and postgraduate programmes based on your Bachelor's degree, academic background and the university's admission criteria.

3. What is the difference between Direct and Fast Track?

Direct Study starts at an overseas university from the first semester. Selected Fast Track programmes begin online in India before continuing at a partner university abroad, subject to credit-transfer approval.

4. How much does it cost to study abroad?

The cost depends on the country, university, course and city. Tuition fees and living expenses should both be included in your budget.

5. Do I need IELTS to study abroad?

It depends on the university and programme. Some institutions accept alternative English tests or other evidence of English proficiency.

6. Can I get a scholarship or education loan?

Scholarships depend on university criteria and your academic profile. Education loans are available through eligible lenders, subject to their approval.

7. How long does the admission process take?

It varies by university, intake and destination. Start checking application deadlines and documentation requirements well before your intended intake.

8. Can I work after completing my degree abroad?

Some countries provide post-study work options to eligible graduates. The rules and duration depend on the destination country's current immigration policy.
